How the detector works

For each topic, PrepPilot calculates correct answers ÷ attempted answers. Topics below 60% accuracy are marked “Needs practice”; topics at 80% or above are marked “On track.” Topics with no attempts are labelled “Not assessed.” These are learning indicators, not a prediction of your exam result.
